About Me

I recently graduated with a degree in Electrical & Electronic Engineering and gained direct industry experience monitoring live Tier-1 enterprise infrastructure at Huawei. That experience taught me firsthand how critical network stability, observability, and incident response are. It also showed me that whether you are running a telecom core, a banking database, or a cloud platform, the core requirement is always the same: zero downtime.

Currently, I hold an AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner certification and am building a strong foundation in Linux systems administration, containerization with Docker, and CI/CD pipelines. My networking foundation covers TCP/IP and UDP suites, essential services like DNS and DHCP, and hands-on exposure to VLAN configuration and basic routing.. My goal is to bridge the gap between legacy telecom infrastructure and modern cloud architectures. I write about this transition in my Blogs.
